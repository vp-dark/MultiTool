Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 8, 2026

Welcome to RealMultiTool ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use our website (realmultitool.com) and our suite of free online utilities.

1. How We Process Your Files (Client-Side Processing)

RealMultiTool is designed with your privacy as the highest priority. Our Image Resizer, File Converter, and other core tools operate entirely on the "client-side." This means that when you upload an image or file, it is processed directly by your web browser on your own device.

  • We do not upload your files to our servers.
  • We do not store, view, or analyze your files.
  • Once you close your browser or refresh the page, all processed data is completely cleared from your active session.

2. General Website Analytics

We may use basic, anonymized web analytics to understand how many people visit our site and which tools are the most popular. This data includes general information like browser type, device type, and referring pages. It does not include personally identifiable information or the contents of any files you process.

3. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
